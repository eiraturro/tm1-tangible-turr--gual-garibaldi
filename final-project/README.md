## The brief

Design and build a **playful tangible prototype**: a physical object that people want to touch, move or approach, and that answers them. Work in your group of 3–4 from session 7 to demo day.

Your project must:

- Sense a person with at least one input (button, knob, distance, light, knock, RFID…)
- Decide what to do with logic and states (`switch/case`, `millis()`)
- Answer with at least one output (light, movement, sound…)
- Live inside a finished object, not on a bare breadboard
- Work on demo day without anyone from the team helping or resetting it

## Materials you can use

| Category | Available |
| --- | --- |
| Board | Arduino Uno, USB cable, breadboard, jumper wires |
| Inputs | Push buttons, potentiometers, LDR, HC-SR04 distance sensor, piezo disc, sound sensor, DHT11, RC522 RFID reader and tags |
| Outputs | LEDs, WS2812B LED strip, SG90 micro servo, DC motor (with transistor or driver), buzzer |
| Electronics | Resistors (220 Ω, 330 Ω, 10 kΩ, 1 MΩ), 1000 µF capacitor, diodes |
| Power | USB power bank, battery pack, 5 V adapter |
| Making | Cardboard, MDF, 3D printing (send parts by session 9), hot glue, tape, zip ties, Velcro |
